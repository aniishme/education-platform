# Course content and video credits

The seed curriculum contains original StudyFlow explanations, worked examples,
practice labs and capstone prompts. No video transcripts or third-party articles
are copied into lesson notes. Course owners listed in the LMS are demonstration
educators; they are **not** the creators of the companion YouTube videos.

The linked videos are external companion resources published on
[freeCodeCamp.org's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@freecodecamp).
Creator attribution, video descriptions and original chapters remain on YouTube.
Computer Science Foundations uses the Harvard CS50 university course resource.
StudyFlow is not affiliated with freeCodeCamp or Harvard.

Use the video's chapter list to find the topic relevant to a lesson. These
full-course resources are shared across lessons; StudyFlow does not claim that
each lesson has a distinct recording. Study-time estimates describe the guided
reading/exercise workload and do not represent the video length. Some recordings
use older tool versions, so consult current official documentation when adapting
examples. Educators can replace any video with a more suitable resource.

The player follows [YouTube's documented iframe/playlist parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters)
and uses the privacy-enhanced embed host. Playlists remain playlists in the
embedded player; they are not scraped or imported into LMS lesson records.
YouTube controls availability, advertisements, branding and embedding permission.
The browser tests verify URL validation, safe embed construction and LMS flows;
they do not claim to verify externally hosted video playback.
